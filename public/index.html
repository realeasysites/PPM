Why steel

A building you won’t be fixing every spring.

No rot, no bugs

Steel won’t rot, warp, or feed carpenter ants and termites like a wood-framed building will.

Open floor space

Clear-span framing means no posts in the way of your trucks, tractors, or lifts.

Low maintenance

Painted steel panels hold up for decades without scraping and repainting.

Won’t burn

Steel frames and panels are non-combustible, which helps protect what’s inside.

Questions

Good to know before you build.

Is the concrete slab included?

No. Concrete, earthwork, and site prep are handled by the owner. We’ll give you the building’s foundation requirements so your concrete contractor knows exactly what to pour.

Is installation included?

For most of our garages and shops, yes: delivery and installation are included in the price. Large red iron buildings are usually sold as a delivered kit, and we can quote installation separately.

Do you deliver to New Hampshire?

Yes. We build throughout Maine and New Hampshire. Freight is priced by distance, so send your ZIP code with your quote request.

Will it hold up to Maine snow?

Our garages and shops are rated for a 90 PSF snow load and 115 MPH winds, with a 4/12 roof pitch that helps shed snow.

Do I get stamped plans for my permit?

Stamped engineered plans are included with our red iron buildings and many of our garage packages. Ask when you request your quote and we’ll confirm what’s included for your building.

Can I add insulation?

Yes. We offer double bubble reflective insulation, Prodex 5mm, and fiberglass. Fully insulated packages are available on popular sizes.

Is there a warranty?

Yes. Coverage depends on the building. Ask about warranty terms for your specific building when you request a quote.
